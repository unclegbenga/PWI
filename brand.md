# Brand — Promise Wesley Initiative

## Source of truth

Promise Wesley Initiative Brand Guide, first edition, October 2026.

## Positioning

PWI is a youth-development initiative building practical pathways for young people to grow with purpose, skills, support, and opportunity. The public-facing voice is ambitious but grounded, warm, direct, practical, and honest about what is still being built.

## Visual tokens

- Black: `#000000`
- Ink: `#151515`
- Gold: `#F5A000`
- Gold dark: `#B96D00`
- Silver: `#A7A9AC`
- Warm white: `#FFF9F0`
- Display: Sora, bold or semibold
- Body: Inter, regular or medium

Gold is used as a purposeful accent rather than a body-text colour. Use the supplied vertical logo intact on a clean black background; never crop, recolour, redraw, or shrink it below 150px wide.
